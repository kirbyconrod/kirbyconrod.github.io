While singular 'they' is the nonbinary language feature that English speakers
tend to be most familiar with — and have the strongest opinions about
— neopronouns (such as 'zie' or 'fae') are also widespread. The very fact that
they're less widely known gives us the opportunity to study questions about
language change: who has already adopted them? and how do new adopters learn
them?

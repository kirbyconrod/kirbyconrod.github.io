I study 

- Syntax: the patterns we follow when we combine words and phrases 
- Sociolinguistics: the way those patterns vary in different communities 
- Pragmatics: the ways they can be appropriate or inappropriate in different contexts 

I focus on linguistic expressions of trans and nonbinary identity, and in
particular on singular 'they' and neopronouns (like 'ze' or 'fae'). These give
us new information about the syntax of pronouns, the sociolinguistic evolution
of pronoun systems, and the ways that languages express respect. 

I'm also interested in teaching and research methodology, and I'm involved in projects on how to effectively teach syntax and how to handle demographic data respectfully in research.

English pronouns can be used in more ways than traditional grammar recognizes.
'They' can refer to a single specific person. 'She' can refer to men; 'he' can
be coreferential with 'cowgirl'. Pronouns can be modified ('you who make me
smile'), used as predicates ('that's a she'), or even as nouns ('a couple of
they/thems'). Even if some varieties of English don't use pronouns in these
ways, we need a theory of syntax that accounts for the varieties that do.

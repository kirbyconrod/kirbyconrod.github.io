Syntax is studied in many ways and taught in many ways. Teaching practices
affect who learns syntax and what they gain from it. The [SynTeach
project](https://blogs.kent.ac.uk/synteach/), which I'm a collaborator in,
studies how syntax is taught at institutions of higher learning in the United
States, the United Kingdom, and Canada, and why it matters.

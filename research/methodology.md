Research methods aren't socially or morally neutral. The way we
categorize the people we study can help us see them in clearer,
more just, and more ethical ways, or it can perpetuate systems of
injustice. My interest in trans linguistics has led me to study this
issue more broadly, not just with respect to gender but also
with respect to race and ethnicity.

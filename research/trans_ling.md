During my graduate training, I started encountering other linguists' ideas
about trans and nonbinary people's language practices, and noticing the ways
that those ideas ran counter to my own experience as a trans nonbinary person.
I don't think there needs to be conflict here — I think good research practices
and respect for trans language go hand in hand, and I aim to share that
belief with other linguists.

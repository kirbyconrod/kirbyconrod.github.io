New developments in the English pronoun system, and other trans-related ways of
using language, have been unevenly adopted. Some people use them effortlessly,
some make a point of using them with effort, and some don't use them at all.
I use sociolinguistic interviews, surveys, and corpus methods to find patterns
in who falls into which of these categories.
